Everything you need to take back your inbox

Built for people drowning in newsletters, promos, and notifications.

See who's filling your inbox

Inbox Janitor scans your mailbox and groups every message by sender, ranked by volume — so the worst offenders rise to the top instantly.

  • Sender grouping with live message counts
  • Search by name or address
  • Sort by most mail, most recent, or A–Z

Act in bulk — archive, delete, unsubscribe

Select as many senders as you like and clean them all at once, with a live progress bar the whole way through.

  • One-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) where supported
  • Bulk archive or delete across many senders
  • Cancel any operation mid-run

Safe by default

Every action shows the exact message count before it runs. Archive is the default, and "delete" only moves mail to Deleted Items — never a permanent purge.

  • Confirmation step before anything happens
  • Recoverable actions only
  • Sign out wipes all local data instantly

Up and running in four steps

No accounts to create, nothing to configure.

1

Open the panel

Click the Inbox Janitor toolbar icon to open the side panel.

2

Sign in

Connect securely with your Microsoft account — no passwords stored.

3

Review senders

See your inbox grouped and ranked by who sends the most.

4

Clean up

Select senders and archive, delete, or unsubscribe in one click.

Your mail never leaves your browser

Inbox Janitor talks only to Microsoft's official Graph API using your own secure token. No email content is ever sent to us — there is no "us" server to send it to. No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
